    What success looks like: you pasted all three values into the app Setup tab and Test Blofin Credentials said success.

  9. 9
    Save credentials and write the compendium file

    Saving writes the encrypted credentials locally. Writing the compendium creates the file Hermes reads.

    What success looks like: the app says Saved & compendium written or ✓ Written.

    Fund Blofin from your debit card

FAQ

I am new to crypto. Is this for me?

Yes. The app includes a How-To tab that explains each step. You can follow along even if you have never used a trading bot or AI assistant before.

I only have a debit card. Can I still do this?

Yes. Use Coinbase to buy crypto with your debit card, then send it to Blofin. The How-To tab includes exact steps.

Do I need a VPN?

BloFin restricts some jurisdictions, so a VPN may be required for platform access from your region. The How-To tab includes a free ProtonVPN routine.

Do I have to place trades myself?

No. After setup, the assistant can trade on your behalf. You can inspect logs and the dashboard anytime.

Where do my API keys go?

They stay on your machine. The app stores credentials locally and syncs them into your local Hermes setup.

What if I need help?

Open the How-To tab inside the app. It walks through ProtonVPN, Coinbase, Blofin, Hermes setup, API keys, and keeping your computer awake.

Is this a subscription or hosted service?

This is a local app with guided setup. The $47/month membership unlocks the app and ongoing updates. There is no ongoing account with us beyond the membership and the crypto exchange accounts you control.

Why is this worth $47/month?

Yes. Active membership is required to use KnightTrader BloFin. If your subscription ends, the app will alert you and stop working until membership is renewed.

What do I do if a step fails?

Read the exact message. Most failures are one missing field, one wrong network, or one missed confirmation. Fix that exact item before continuing.

Can I watch every trade?

Yes. Use the Trading tab for live equity, open positions, and recent activity. Use Logs for exact output. Use the Hermes dashboard for detailed assistant messages.
